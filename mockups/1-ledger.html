What stops a student finishing school

Three pressures come up again and again in the applications we read. SFS is built to answer all three at once, because solving one alone rarely keeps a student enrolled.

i

Educational barriers

Students face interruptions from financial, social and geographical pressures — a family move, a long commute, a year out to earn — that quietly end an education without anyone recording why.

ii

Financial pressure

Fees are only part of it. Transport, study materials, exam costs and a device with a data connection add up to more than most families can absorb in a single year.

iii

Staying the course

Money alone does not finish a degree. Mentoring, family engagement and steady monitoring are what carry a student through the years after the first cheque clears.
