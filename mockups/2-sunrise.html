What we solve

Three things stand between a student and their final exam

These come up in almost every application we read. SFS is built to answer all three together, because fixing only one rarely keeps a student in school.

Education interrupted

A family move, a long daily commute, a year taken out to earn. Studies stop quietly, and usually nobody records why.

Money runs short

Fees are only the start. Transport, books, exam costs and a phone with data add up past what most families can manage.

Nobody to lean on

Money alone doesn't finish a degree. Mentoring and steady check-ins carry a student through the years that follow.
