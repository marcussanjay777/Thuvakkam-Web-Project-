What we solve

Three pressures that quietly end an education

They appear in nearly every application we read. SFS answers all three together, because solving one alone rarely keeps a student enrolled.

Education interrupted

A family move, a long commute, a year taken out to earn. Studies stop quietly, and usually nobody records why.

Money runs short

Fees are only the beginning. Transport, books, exam costs and a device with data push it out of reach.

Nobody to lean on

Money alone does not finish a degree. Mentoring and steady check-ins carry a student through the years after.
