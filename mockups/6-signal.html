The problem

Three things end an education

They show up in nearly every application we read. SFS answers all three at once, because fixing one alone rarely keeps a student in school.

Studies stop

A family move, a long commute, a year taken out to earn. It ends quietly and nobody writes down why.

Money runs out

Fees are only the start. Transport, books, exams and a phone with data push it out of reach.

No one to ask

Money alone doesn't finish a degree. Mentoring and check-ins carry a student through the years after.
